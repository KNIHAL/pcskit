# Code of conduct

We want PCS to be a place where people can ask questions, report bugs and send code without being treated badly. This follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith and be patient with newcomers.
- Give and accept feedback on work kindly and specifically.
- Respect other people's privacy. Do not post private information without permission.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualised language or attention.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or repeated off-topic disruption.
- Sharing someone's private information.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting (Security tab > Report a vulnerability) and write "conduct" in the title, or contact the maintainer, @KNIHAL, through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately and fairly.
